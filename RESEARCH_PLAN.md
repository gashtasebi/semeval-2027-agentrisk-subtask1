# Research Plan

## 1. Research Topic

Risk Level Estimation for Agentic Interactions in SemEval 2027
Task 10: AgentRisk, Subtask 1.

## 2. Problem Statement

The project investigates methods for estimating the severity of
risks associated with proposed agent actions, taking into account
the preceding interaction trajectory.

## 3. Research Objectives

1. Understand the official task formulation and dataset.
2. Establish reproducible baseline results.
3. Compare different modelling approaches.
4. Investigate the contribution of interaction context.
5. Analyse prediction errors and model limitations.
6. Prepare a valid competition submission and a research report.

## 4. Initial Research Questions

RQ1: How well do simple baselines estimate risk severity?

RQ2: Does interaction context improve risk estimation?

RQ3: How does an ordinal-aware model compare with conventional
classification or regression approaches?

RQ4: Which risk levels and interaction patterns are most difficult
to predict?

## 5. Methodology

Phase 1: Official task and literature review  
Phase 2: Dataset inspection and quality checks  
Phase 3: Baseline implementation  
Phase 4: Model development and controlled experiments  
Phase 5: Error analysis and ablation studies  
Phase 6: Final evaluation and submission  
Phase 7: Research report and system documentation

## 6. Evaluation

Use the official evaluation metric and protocol specified by the
organisers. MAE is the initial candidate metric, subject to
verification against the official instructions.

## 7. Reproducibility

All experiments will be linked to code commits and documented with
their data version, configuration, evaluation results, and limitations.

## 8. Scope and Limitations

The project focuses exclusively on Subtask 1. Any additional
research questions will be prioritised according to available time,
data, computational resources, and the official competition rules.

## 9. Research Status

This plan is provisional. Changes to the research questions,
methodology, and evaluation protocol will be recorded in
DECISION_LOG.md.
